Projects

Here are some of my projects:

  • DIY Arduino Obstacle Avoider

  • A DIY Arduino Obstacle Avoider is a small autonomous robot that uses an Arduino Uno and an HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor to detect obstacles in its path. When an object is detected, it automatically changes direction using a motor driver, allowing it to move without human control.

  • Non-Invasive Diagnosis and Therapy for Sleep Paralysis and other Sleep Disorders

  • Non-Invasive Diagnosis and Therapy for Sleep Paralysis and Other Sleep Disorders focuses on identifying and managing conditions like Sleep Paralysis using safe, non-surgical methods. Diagnosis involves sleep tracking, questionnaires, and tools like Polysomnography, while therapy includes improving sleep habits, stress management, and techniques such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia. These approaches aim to enhance sleep quality without medication or invasive procedures.

  • Lithium gas detecting system

  • A lithium gas detecting system is a safety device designed to identify the presence of lithium gas, which can be hazardous. It typically uses sensors to monitor air quality and detect lithium gas leaks, providing early warning of potential hazards.
